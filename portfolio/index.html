🔬 Research Study • Explainable AI

Explainable Machine Learning for Early Cardiovascular Disease Risk Prediction

Golla Raghavendra • MSc Data Science, Chandigarh University

📄 Abstract

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) remain the leading cause of global mortality. While complex machine learning architectures achieve high predictive accuracy, their clinical adoption is hindered by their "black-box" nature. This research develops a transparent predictive framework integrating SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) with gradient boosting ensembles. The system quantifies individual clinical biomarkers, generates global risk feature rankings, and provides clinician-interpretable waterfall explanations for high-stakes decision support.

Key Contributions

  • Model Benchmarking: Comparative performance analysis across Random Forest, XGBoost, and HistGradientBoosting classifiers.
  • Explainability Layer: Game-theoretic SHAP tree explainers translating non-linear feature interactions into clinical impact scores.
  • Decision Support: Individualized risk factor attributions tailored for medical practitioner review.
